# Chapter 11: Taxation of Equity Products — Short Notes

## 🎯 What you'll learn
- How **listed and unlisted equity shares** are taxed (dividend + capital gains).
- The big three sections: **STCG @20% (Sec 111A)**, **LTCG @12.50% over ₹1.25 lakh (Sec 112A)**, **LTCG @12.50% (Sec 112)**.
- The **grandfathering** rule for shares bought on/before 31-01-2018.
- Preference shares, share warrants, equity-oriented mutual funds.
- **Derivatives (F&O)** as business income; **bonus stripping** (Sec 94(8)).

> An **equity share** = part-ownership of a company. You earn two ways: **dividend** (a slice of profits) and **capital gain** (selling higher than you bought). Equity is riskier than debt, and its tax rules are different.

---

## 11.1 Sources of Income

| Income | When it arises | How taxed |
|---|---|---|
| **Dividend** | Company pays out profits | Slab rate (resident); special rate (non-resident) |
| **Capital gain** | Sell the share above cost | STCG or LTCG depending on holding period |

**Dividend timing:** Final dividend = income of the year it's *declared* at the AGM (receipt date irrelevant). Interim dividend = income of the year it's *made available* (receipt basis).

Since the **Dividend Distribution Tax was abolished (Finance Act 2020, from FY 2020-21)**, dividends are taxed in the *shareholder's* hands.

**Capital gains formula:**
Full Value of Consideration − (transfer expenses + cost of acquisition + cost of improvement) − exemptions (Sec 54–54GB) = Capital Gain.

LTCG generally @ **12.50%**; STCG generally added to income at slab rates (but **20%** under Sec 111A in certain cases).

## 11.2 Listed Equity Shares

### 11.2.1 Charges & taxes when trading
| Charge | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Brokerage** | Broker's fee |
| **Securities Transaction Tax (STT)** | Tax on buy/sell of securities (not debt); collected by exchange, paid to Govt by the 7th of next month |
| **Stamp Duty** | On transfer; unified rates across India from 01-04-2020 for listed securities |
| **Exchange charges** | Levied by exchange on both sides |
| **SEBI Turnover charges** | ₹10 per crore, both sides |
| **DP charges** | Depository (NSDL/CDSL) fee on debit of shares + GST |
| **GST** | 18% on brokerage + transaction + clearing charges |

### 11.2.2 Tax on dividend
- **Resident:** slab rates; interest-expense deduction capped at **20% of dividend**, nothing else.
- **Non-resident / foreign company / FPI:** special rate (subject to DTAA), **no deduction**, **no Chapter VIA (80C–80U)**. Most DTAAs tax dividend in source country at **5%–15%**.

### 11.2.3 Period of holding
- **Listed shares:** short-term if held **≤12 months**; otherwise long-term.
- **Demat shares:** period determined by **FIFO** (First-In-First-Out) — Section 45(2A) — applied **account-wise**.
- **Physical shares sold through broker:** date of broker's note = date of transfer (if followed by delivery).

**💡 FIFO example:** If shares entered the demat account on different dates and you sell some, the *earliest-acquired* are treated as sold first.

### 11.2.4 LTCG under Section 112A
- LTCG on **listed equity shares is exempt up to ₹1,25,000** per year; the **excess is taxed @12.50%** (+ surcharge + cess).
- Condition: **STT paid on acquisition AND transfer** (with listed exceptions — IFSC transactions in foreign currency, shares before 01-10-2004, court/SEBI/RBI-approved acquisitions, ESOP/ESPS, etc.).

#### Grandfathering (shares bought on/before 31-01-2018)
Cost of acquisition = **higher of**:
- (a) actual cost; OR
- (b) **lower of** [FMV on 31-01-2018] and [sale consideration].

FMV = highest quoted price on 31-01-2018 (or the last traded day before).

**💡 The four scenarios (cost ₹100, FMV ₹200 on 31-01-2018):**
| Scenario | Sale price | Cost taken | Gain/Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹250 | ₹200 (FMV) | LTCG ₹50 |
| 2 | ₹150 | ₹150 (sale, since < FMV) | **NIL** (grandfathering can't manufacture a loss) |
| 3 (FMV ₹50) | ₹150 | ₹100 (actual, since FMV < cost) | LTCG ₹50 |
| 4 | ₹50 | ₹100 (actual) | LTCG **loss ₹50** |

⚠️ **Trap:** grandfathering never turns a real gain (sale > cost) into a loss just because sale < FMV.

### 11.2.5 LTCG under Section 112
LTCG on equity **not** covered by 112A (e.g., off-market sale, no STT, or unlisted shares) is taxed @**12.50%** — but **without the ₹1.25 lakh exemption**.

### 11.2.6 STCG under Section 111A
- STCG on listed equity (STT paid) → **20%**.
- No deduction under 80C–80U against this STCG.

### 11.2.7 Normal STCG
If conditions of 111A aren't met (e.g., no STT) → STCG taxed at **normal slab rates**.

**Table 11.1 — Listed equity capital gains:**
| Nature | STT paid | STT NOT paid |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term | 12.50% (first ₹1.25 lakh exempt) | 12.50% |
| Short-term | 20% | slab rate |

**Surcharge on 111A/112/112A capital gains** is capped: Nil up to ₹50 lakh; 10% (₹50L–1cr); **15% above ₹1 crore** (does not rise to 25%/37% like other income).

**💡 Worked example (Mr. X):** 1,000 ABC shares bought 01-06-2023 @₹105, sold 24-07-2024 @₹107 → held 13+ months → **LTCG ₹2,000 @12.50% (within ₹1.25L so no tax)**. 1,000 XYZ bought 01-10-2023 @₹120, sold 01-08-2024 @₹135 → held 10 months → **STCG ₹15,000 @20% (Sec 111A)**.

## 11.3 Unlisted Equity Shares
- **Short-term if held ≤24 months; long-term if >24 months.**
- **LTCG @12.50%** (+ surcharge/cess); **STCG at slab rates**.
- Dividend taxed same as listed shares.
- If unlisted shares are sold in an **IPO**, the gain is taxed under **Section 112A**.

**💡 Worked example:** Same ABC/XYZ shares but unlisted, both sold within 24 months → both are **short-term, taxed at slab rates** (not 20%, because not listed/STT).

---

## 11.4 Preference Shares
Preference shares = priority on **fixed dividend** and **repayment on winding-up**.
- Dividend taxed like equity shares.
- **Listed:** long-term after 12 months. **Unlisted:** after 24 months. LTCG @12.50%, STCG at applicable rates.

**💡 Worked example:** 1,000 listed pref shares @₹105 (01-07-2018), sold @₹120 (25-07-2024). Held >12 months → LTCG = ₹15,000 @12.50% = **₹1,875 tax**.

## 11.5 Share Warrants
A **share warrant** gives the holder the right (not obligation) to buy shares at a fixed **strike price** later. Holder pays ≥25% upfront; tenure ≤18 months. If not exercised within 3 months of full payment, the money is **forfeited**.

Three possible events:
1. **Conversion into shares** = treated as transfer of the warrant. Gain = FMV of shares (Sec 50D) − strike price. STCG if held ≤12 months (listed), LTCG @12.50% if >12 months.
2. **Transfer of the warrant** = transfer of capital asset. Gain = sale price − upfront price paid.
3. **Forfeiture** = the loss is **ignored** (no tax treatment).

**💡 Worked example:** 1,00,000 warrants @₹200 (strike) bought 29-12-2023, ₹50 lakh paid upfront (25%).
- *Scenario 1 (exercised 29-03-2025, FMV ₹250):* held >12 months → **LTCG = (250−200)×1,00,000 = ₹50,00,000 @12.50%**.
- *Scenario 2 (transferred to Mr. Y @₹75, 15-08-2024):* held <12 months → **STCG = (75−50)×1,00,000 = ₹25,00,000 at slab rate**.

## 11.6 Equity-Oriented Mutual Funds

**Equity-Oriented Fund** = a fund (or qualifying ULIP that loses 10(10D) exemption) where:
- (a) if it invests via another exchange-traded fund: ≥90% in that fund, which itself invests ≥90% in listed domestic equity; OR
- (b) otherwise: ≥**65%** in listed domestic equity shares.

Key MF terms: **FoF** (fund of funds), **ELSS** (tax-saving, 3-yr lock-in, 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh, old regime), **SIP** (regular investing), **SWP** (regular withdrawal — gains taxable), **STP** (transfer between schemes of same AMC).

### Tax treatment
- **Dividend:** resident slab (20%-of-dividend interest deduction); non-resident/foreign co. flat **20%** (10% if from IFSC) + surcharge/cess, subject to DTAA.
- **LTCG (Sec 112A):** units long-term after **12 months**; **exempt up to ₹1,25,000**, excess @12.50% (needs STT on transfer, unless IFSC foreign-currency).
- **Grandfathering** for units bought on/before 31-01-2018, same higher-of mechanism.
- **STCG (Sec 111A):** **20%** if STT paid; else slab.

**💡 Worked example (grandfathering):** 5,000 units bought 01-05-2017 @₹200, FMV 31-01-2018 = ₹225, sold 01-08-2024 @₹300.
- Cost = higher of [₹10,00,000 actual] and [lower of (FMV ₹11,25,000) and (sale ₹15,00,000)] = **₹11,25,000**.
- LTCG = 15,00,000 − 11,25,000 = **₹3,75,000 @12.50%** (over ₹1.25 lakh).

**💡 Worked example (dividend + LTCG):** 10,000 units @₹250, dividend ₹50/unit, sold @₹280.
- Dividend ₹5,00,000; interest paid ₹1,60,000 → deduction capped ₹1,00,000 → **taxable dividend ₹4,00,000**.
- LTCG = (280−250)×10,000 = **₹3,00,000 @12.50%** (STT paid).

## 11.7 Derivatives (F&O)

A **derivative** derives its value from an underlying asset. Types: **Futures** (obligation to buy/sell at a future date at a set price), **Options** (right not obligation — Call = buy, Put = sell; buyer pays **premium**), **Forwards** (OTC customized), **Swaps** (exchange cash flows — interest-rate or currency).

### 11.7.2 Nature of derivative income
- F&O gains/losses are **business income (PGBP)** (or other sources), depending on whether you trade as a business.
- F&O is **non-speculative** business (Section 43(5) excludes it from "speculative") even though there's no delivery — *provided* traded on a recognised exchange via a SEBI-registered broker with a time-stamped contract note showing client ID + PAN.
- **Speculative loss** can be set off only against speculative income; **non-speculative F&O loss** can be set off against any normal business income.

### 11.7.3 Turnover (ICAI Guidance Note)
- Turnover = total of **favourable + unfavourable differences** + **premium received on sale of options** + reverse-trade differences.
- Matters for **tax audit** and **presumptive scheme (Sec 44AD)**.

### 11.7.4 Presumptive scheme (Sec 44AD)
- Available if turnover ≤ **₹2 crore (or ₹3 crore)**.
- Declare profit at **6%** of turnover (digital receipts) or 8% (cash). No books, no audit; can pay 100% advance tax by 15 March.

### 11.7.5 Set-off & carry forward
- Non-speculative business loss → set off against any head **except salary**.
- Carry forward up to **8 assessment years**, set off only against **business income**.
- ⚠️ **Must file return by due date** or you lose the carry-forward right.

**💡 Worked example (Mr. X, call option, lot 1,000, strike ₹450, premium ₹35):**
- *Exercised then sold @₹550:* Business income = 550,000 − (450+35)×1,000 = **₹65,000**.
- *Transferred @₹30:* Loss = (30−35)×1,000 = **−₹5,000**.
- *Transferred @₹37:* Gain = (37−35)×1,000 = **₹2,000**.
- *Expired (premium ₹10):* Loss = (10−35)×1,000 = **−₹25,000**.

All taxed/set off as normal business income.

## 11.8 Bonus Stripping (Section 94(8))
**The trick:** Buy units just before the record date, get bonus units, then sell the original units cheaply to book an artificial short-term loss (while keeping the free bonus units).

**The rule:** If you (a) buy securities/units within **3 months before** the record date, (b) get bonus units, and (c) sell the original within **9 months after** the record date while still holding the bonus units — the **loss on the original is ignored**. That ignored loss becomes the **cost of acquisition of the bonus units**.

**💡 Worked example:** 1,000 shares @₹106 (01-07-2023), 1:1 bonus on 01-09-2023, original 1,000 sold @₹95 (01-04-2024).
- Loss = (106−95)×1,000 = **₹11,000 ignored**.
- Becomes cost of the 1,000 bonus shares → cost per bonus share = **₹11**.

## 11.9 Benefits NOT allowed from capital gains
- **No deduction under 80C–80U** from LTCG @12.50% (Sec 112/112A) or STCG @20% (Sec 111A).
- **No foreign-currency computation** for the 12.50% LTCG.
- **No Section 87A rebate** against 112A LTCG (rebate allowed only on the rest of total income).

## 11.10 Adjustment of exemption limit
A resident individual/HUF can use their **basic exemption limit** to absorb part of their 111A/112/112A capital gains, if other income falls short of the limit.

**💡 Example:** Other income ₹1,85,000; LTCG on unlisted shares ₹2,50,000. Basic exemption ₹2,50,000. Shortfall ₹65,000 absorbs ₹65,000 of LTCG → only **₹1,85,000 of LTCG is taxed**.

## 📌 One-Page Recap
- **Listed equity:** LTCG (>12 mth) @12.50% over ₹1.25L (112A, STT); STCG @20% (111A, STT). No STT → 12.50% / slab.
- **Unlisted equity:** LTCG (>24 mth) @12.50% (112); STCG at slab.
- **Grandfathering (≤31-01-2018):** cost = higher of actual vs (lower of FMV-31Jan2018 and sale).
- **Dividend:** resident slab (20% interest deduction); non-resident 20%, no deduction.
- **Warrants:** conversion/transfer = transfer; forfeiture loss ignored.
- **Equity MF:** like listed equity (112A/111A, STT).
- **F&O:** non-speculative business income; loss set off against any head except salary; carry forward 8 yrs (file on time).
- **Bonus stripping (94(8)):** ignored loss shifts to bonus units' cost.
