# Chapter 11: Taxation of Equity Products — Flashcards

Cover the answer, recall it, then check.

**Q1.** What two types of income come from equity investments?
> **A:** **Dividend** (share of company profits) and **capital gains** (selling above cost).

**Q2.** In whose hands is dividend taxed after Finance Act 2020?
> **A:** In the **shareholder's** hands (Dividend Distribution Tax abolished from FY 2020-21).

**Q3.** When is a final dividend treated as income?
> **A:** In the year it is **declared at the AGM** (date of receipt is irrelevant). Interim dividend is on receipt basis.

**Q4.** What is the LTCG rate and annual exemption under Section 112A?
> **A:** LTCG over **₹1,25,000** taxed @**12.50%** (+ surcharge/cess); first ₹1.25 lakh exempt.

**Q5.** What is the STCG rate under Section 111A?
> **A:** **20%** (when STT is paid / IFSC foreign-currency transfer). Else, normal slab rates.

**Q6.** What conditions must be met for Section 112A to apply?
> **A:** STT paid on **both acquisition and transfer** of listed equity (with listed exceptions like IFSC, pre-01-10-2004 shares, ESOPs, court/SEBI/RBI-approved acquisitions).

**Q7.** Holding period to make listed equity shares long-term?
> **A:** More than **12 months**.

**Q8.** Holding period to make unlisted equity shares long-term?
> **A:** More than **24 months**.

**Q9.** How is the demat holding period determined?
> **A:** By **FIFO** (Section 45(2A)) — first shares in are first out — applied **account-wise**.

**Q10.** Grandfathering: how is the cost of a share bought on/before 31-01-2018 found?
> **A:** **Higher of** (a) actual cost; or (b) **lower of** [FMV on 31-01-2018] and [sale consideration].

**Q11.** Share bought @₹100, FMV 31-01-2018 = ₹200, sold @₹150. What's the LTCG?
> **A:** Cost = lower of (₹200 FMV, ₹150 sale) = ₹150 → LTCG = 150−150 = **NIL** (grandfathering won't create a loss).

**Q12.** Share @₹100, FMV ₹200, sold @₹50. Gain/loss?
> **A:** Cost = actual ₹100 (since sale < FMV and < cost) → **LTCG loss ₹50**.

**Q13.** How is LTCG taxed when STT is NOT paid on listed equity (Section 112)?
> **A:** **12.50%**, but **without** the ₹1.25 lakh exemption.

**Q14.** How is dividend taxed for a non-resident?
> **A:** At a **special rate** (typically 20%, 10% if IFSC) subject to DTAA, with **no deduction** and **no Chapter VIA (80C–80U)**.

**Q15.** What is STT and who collects it?
> **A:** **Securities Transaction Tax** on buy/sell of securities (not debt), collected by the exchange/MF trustee/merchant banker and paid to Govt by the 7th of the next month.

**Q16.** Long-term holding period for preference shares (listed vs unlisted)?
> **A:** Listed: **12 months**; Unlisted: **24 months**. LTCG @12.50%.

**Q17.** What is a share warrant?
> **A:** A right (not obligation) to buy shares at a fixed **strike price** later; holder pays ≥25% upfront, tenure ≤18 months.

**Q18.** How is conversion of a share warrant into shares treated?
> **A:** As a **transfer of the warrant**. Gain = FMV of shares (Sec 50D) − strike price; STCG if ≤12 months, LTCG @12.50% if >12 months.

**Q19.** What is the tax treatment of forfeiture of warrant premium?
> **A:** The loss is **ignored** — no tax treatment.

**Q20.** Define an Equity-Oriented Fund.
> **A:** A fund investing ≥**65%** in listed domestic equity (or via ETF route ≥90%/≥90%); qualifying ULIPs without 10(10D) exemption also count.

**Q21.** Tax treatment of equity-oriented MF units — STCG and LTCG?
> **A:** **STCG @20%** (Sec 111A, STT) and **LTCG @12.50%** over ₹1.25 lakh (Sec 112A), long-term after 12 months.

**Q22.** What is ELSS and its key features?
> **A:** Equity Linked Savings Scheme — 80C deduction up to **₹1.5 lakh** (old regime), **3-year lock-in** (shortest among tax-savers).

**Q23.** Under which head is F&O income taxed?
> **A:** **Profits and Gains of Business or Profession (PGBP)** (or other sources) — treated as **non-speculative** business under Section 43(5).

**Q24.** Why is exchange-traded F&O treated as non-speculative despite no delivery?
> **A:** Section 43(5) specifically excludes it, provided it's on a recognised exchange via a SEBI-registered broker with a time-stamped contract note (client ID + PAN).

**Q25.** How is F&O turnover computed (ICAI Guidance Note)?
> **A:** Total of favourable + unfavourable differences + premium received on sale of options + reverse-trade differences.

**Q26.** Under Section 44AD presumptive scheme, what profit can an F&O trader declare?
> **A:** **6%** of turnover (digital receipts) or 8% (cash), if turnover ≤ ₹2 crore (or ₹3 crore). No books, no audit.

**Q27.** Against what can a non-speculative F&O business loss be set off, and for how long carried forward?
> **A:** Any head **except salary** in the same year; carried forward **8 assessment years**, set off only against business income (return must be filed on time).

**Q28.** What is bonus stripping and which section curbs it?
> **A:** Buying just before a bonus record date and selling the original at a loss for an artificial deduction. **Section 94(8)** ignores that loss.

**Q29.** Under Section 94(8), what is the 3-month/9-month rule?
> **A:** Buy within **3 months before** record date, get bonus, sell original within **9 months after** while still holding bonus → loss ignored, added to bonus units' cost.

**Q30.** 1,000 shares @₹106, 1:1 bonus, original sold @₹95. What is the cost of the bonus shares under 94(8)?
> **A:** Ignored loss = (106−95)×1,000 = ₹11,000 → cost of 1,000 bonus shares = **₹11,000 (₹11/share)**.

**Q31.** Is the Section 87A rebate available against 112A LTCG?
> **A:** **No** — rebate is allowed only on tax for the rest of total income, not on 112A LTCG.

**Q32.** What is the surcharge cap on capital gains under 111A/112/112A above ₹1 crore?
> **A:** **15%** (does not climb to 25%/37% as it does for other income).

**Q33.** Can a resident individual use the basic exemption limit against capital gains?
> **A:** **Yes** — if other income is below the basic exemption limit, the shortfall reduces the 111A/112/112A gains chargeable to tax.
