# Chapter 12: Taxation of Other Products — Flashcards

Cover the answer, recall it, then check.

**Q1.** What are the two tax points for an ESOP?
> **A:** (1) **At allotment** — perquisite taxed as salary; (2) **At sale** — capital gain.

**Q2.** How is the ESOP perquisite computed?
> **A:** (**FMV on the exercise date** − price paid by employee) × number of shares. (Vesting/allotment-date FMV is irrelevant.)

**Q3.** For ESOP shares, what is the cost of acquisition and when does holding start?
> **A:** Cost = **FMV on the exercise date** (already taxed as perquisite); holding period starts from the **date of allotment**.

**Q4.** Who gets the ESOP perquisite-tax deferment?
> **A:** Employees of **eligible start-ups under Section 80-IAC** (company/LLP incorporated 01-04-2016 to before 01-04-2024, turnover ≤₹100 crore, with Inter-Ministerial Board certificate).

**Q5.** What interest do SGBs pay and how is it taxed?
> **A:** **2.50% p.a.** (half-yearly) on nominal value; taxed under *Other Sources* at slab rates; **no TDS**.

**Q6.** Is redemption of an SGB by an individual taxable?
> **A:** **No** — Section 47 says individual redemption (maturity or premature) is not a transfer, so no capital gain.

**Q7.** How is a secondary-market SALE of an SGB taxed?
> **A:** LTCG @**12.50%** if held >12 months; STCG at slab if ≤12 months. (The Section 47 exemption applies only to redemption, not sale.)

**Q8.** When selling an SGB before the interest due date, how is accrued interest handled?
> **A:** It is **carved out** of the sale price and taxed separately as interest; the rest is the capital-gain consideration.

**Q9.** Difference between NPS Tier I and Tier II?
> **A:** **Tier I** = mandatory, locked retirement account. **Tier II** = voluntary, withdraw anytime; taxed as **capital gains** like an open-ended MF.

**Q10.** Deduction for an employee's own NPS contribution (Section 80CCD(1))?
> **A:** Lower of contribution or **10% of salary**, within the ₹1.5 lakh 80C cap.

**Q11.** What does Section 80CCD(1B) provide?
> **A:** An **additional ₹50,000** deduction above the ₹1.5 lakh cap (also covers NPS Vatsalya).

**Q12.** Employer NPS contribution deduction limit under 80CCD(2)?
> **A:** **14%** of salary for Govt employees; **10%** (old regime) or **14%** (new regime) for other employees. Available in both regimes.

**Q13.** Self-employed NPS deduction under 80CCD(1)?
> **A:** Lower of contribution or **20% of gross total income** (+ ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)), old regime.

**Q14.** What is the maximum total NPS-related deduction an individual can claim?
> **A:** **₹2,00,000** (₹1.5 lakh under the 80C/80CCC/80CCD(1) cap + ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)).

**Q15.** How much of the final NPS withdrawal is exempt?
> **A:** **60% of the corpus** (effectively the whole permitted withdrawal is tax-free).

**Q16.** Is the NPS amount received by a nominee on the subscriber's death taxable?
> **A:** **No — fully exempt.**

**Q17.** Above what amount are employer welfare contributions (PF + NPS + Superannuation) taxed as a perquisite?
> **A:** Above **₹7,50,000** per year.

**Q18.** Which REIT incomes get pass-through status (taxed at unit-holder)?
> **A:** **Rental income, interest from SPV, and dividend from SPV.** Capital gains are taxed at the REIT level.

**Q19.** How does InvIT taxation differ from REIT?
> **A:** Same, **except rental income** — taxed in the **InvIT's own hands**, not passed through to unit-holders.

**Q20.** How is redemption of REIT units taxed?
> **A:** Under **Section 56(2)(xii)** as *Income from Other Sources* (not capital gains).

**Q21.** TDS rate (Sec 194LBA) on rental income distributed by REIT to a resident?
> **A:** **10%** (non-resident: 30%; foreign company 35%).

**Q22.** How are Category-I and II AIFs taxed?
> **A:** **Pass-through (Sec 115UB)** — income exempt at AIF and taxed in investors' hands, **except business income** which is taxed at the AIF (MMR if a trust).

**Q23.** How are Category-III AIFs taxed?
> **A:** **No pass-through** — like a normal trust/company/LLP (unless a "specified fund" under 10(4D)).

**Q24.** Can an AIF pass its losses to unit-holders?
> **A:** **Business losses stay with the AIF.** Since AY 2020-21, **non-business losses pass through** (if the unit held ≥12 months).

**Q25.** How is a Gold ETF taxed if held more than 12 months?
> **A:** **LTCG @12.50%.** Held ≤12 months → slab rate. (If bought after 01-04-2023 and sold before 01-04-2025 → always short-term, slab.)

**Q26.** How is an equity index ETF taxed?
> **A:** Like a listed equity MF — **LTCG @12.50% over ₹1.25 lakh (112A)**, **STCG @20% (111A)**.

**Q27.** When are life insurance maturity proceeds taxable (policies on/after 01-04-2012)?
> **A:** When the premium in any year exceeds **10% of the sum assured** (20% for 01-04-2003 to 31-03-2012). Death proceeds are **always exempt**.

**Q28.** For an endowment policy taken on/after 01-04-2023, what makes maturity taxable?
> **A:** If total premium exceeds **₹5,00,000**, proceeds are taxable as *Other Sources* even if the 10% test is met (death proceeds still exempt).

**Q29.** When does a ULIP lose its 10(10D) exemption?
> **A:** High-value ULIPs issued **on/after 01-02-2021** with **aggregate premium > ₹2,50,000** — treated as a capital asset.

**Q30.** How is the equity-oriented fund inside a high-premium ULIP taxed?
> **A:** Long-term period **1 year**; **LTCG @12.50%, STCG @20%**.

**Q31.** Minimum holding period to keep 80C deduction — ULIP vs life policy?
> **A:** **ULIP 5 years; life insurance policy 2 years.** Early termination claws back earlier deductions.

**Q32.** How is reverse mortgage treated under the Income Tax Act?
> **A:** **Section 47(xvi):** not a "transfer" (no capital gain); **Section 10(43):** the loan amount received is **exempt**.
