# Chapter 12: Taxation of Other Products — Short Notes

## 12.1 Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs)

An **ESOP** gives an employee the *right (not obligation)* to buy company shares at a fixed cheap price after a **vesting period**. Common in start-ups that can't pay big salaries.

Key dates: **Grant date** → **Vesting date** (when you may exercise) → **Exercise date** (when you actually buy) → **Allotment** of shares → later **Sale**.

### 12.1.2 Two tax points

**Point 1 — At allotment (taxed as SALARY perquisite):**
> Perquisite = (**FMV on exercise date** − price paid by employee) × number of shares.

⚠️ **Trap:** Use the FMV on the **exercise date**, NOT the vesting date or allotment date.

**💡 Worked example:** FMV on exercise (10-05-2024) = ₹6,500; price paid = ₹500; 100 shares → perquisite = (6,500−500)×100 = **₹6,00,000** taxed as salary.

**Point 2 — At sale (taxed as CAPITAL GAIN):**
- Period of holding starts from the **date of allotment** (not exercise).
- **Cost of acquisition = FMV on the exercise date** (the amount already taxed as perquisite).

Capital gain treatment:
| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Listed, STT paid, >12 mth (LTCG) | Sec 112A: 12.50% over ₹1.25 lakh |
| Listed, STT paid, ≤12 mth (STCG) | Sec 111A: 20% |
| Unlisted, no STT, >24 mth (LTCG) | Sec 112: 12.50% |
| Unlisted, no STT, ≤24 mth (STCG) | slab rate |

### 12.1.3 Start-up deferment
For **eligible start-ups under Section 80-IAC**, tax on the ESOP **perquisite is deferred** (TDS/payment delayed). Eligible start-up = company/LLP incorporated **01-04-2016 to before 01-04-2024**, turnover **≤₹100 crore**, with an Inter-Ministerial Board certificate.

## 12.2 Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)

SGBs are **government securities denominated in grams of gold** (issued by RBI). A paper substitute for physical gold — no storage/making-charge worries.

Key facts: **2.50% p.a. interest** (half-yearly) on nominal value; tenure **8 years**; premature redemption allowed after **5th year**; limit **4 kg/individual, 4 kg/HUF, 20 kg/trust** per fiscal year.

### 12.2.1 Tax
- **Interest:** taxed under *Other Sources* at slab rates; **no TDS** (it's a Govt security).
- **Redemption by an individual (maturity or premature):** **NOT taxed** — Section 47 says individual redemption is *not a transfer*. So no capital gain.
- **Redemption by a non-individual** (trust etc.): LTCG @12.50% if held >12 months, else slab.
- **Sale/transfer in secondary market** (any holder, incl. individual): LTCG @12.50% if >12 months; STCG at slab if ≤12 months. **The exemption applies only to redemption, not to sale.**

**💡 Worked example:** Mr. X buys SGBs for ₹5 lakh, redeems for ₹6 lakh. Gain **not taxed** (he's an individual, redemption ≠ transfer). But interest each year is taxed.

**💡 Worked example (secondary-market sale):** Bought 100 SGBs @₹4,25,000 (28-04-2019), sold 01-08-2024 @₹7,50,000 (before the interest due date).
- Accrued interest 29-04 to 01-08 (95 days) = 4,25,000 × 2.5% × 95/365 = ₹2,765 → carved out, taxed as interest.
- Sale price for capital gain = 7,50,000 − 2,765 = ₹7,47,235.
- LTCG = 7,47,235 − 4,25,000 = **₹3,22,235 @12.50%**.

⚠️ If sold **right after** the interest due date, no interest accrues to carve out → full sale price used.

## 12.3 National Pension System (NPS)

A **defined-contribution retirement scheme** (PFRDA-regulated). Two accounts:
- **Tier I:** mandatory retirement account (locked).
- **Tier II:** voluntary, withdraw anytime; taxed like an open-ended mutual fund (capital gains).

### 12.3.1 Deductions on contribution
| Contributor | Section | Deduction |
|---|---|---|
| **Employee's own** | 80CCD(1) | Lower of contribution or **10% of salary**; inside the ₹1.5 lakh 80C cap |
| **Additional (any individual)** | 80CCD(1B) | Extra **₹50,000** above the ₹1.5 lakh cap |
| **Employer's contribution** | 80CCD(2) | **14%** of salary (Govt employees); **10%** (other employees, old regime) or **14%** (new regime) |
| **Self-employed** | 80CCD(1) | Lower of contribution or **20% of gross income** (+ ₹50,000 under 1B) |
| **NPS Vatsalya (minor)** | 80CCD(1B) | Parent/guardian, within ₹50,000 cap |

💡 **Salary** here = basic + DA (if part of retirement benefits). **80C + 80CCC + 80CCD(1) together capped at ₹1,50,000**; with 80CCD(1B)'s ₹50,000 → total NPS-related deduction can reach **₹2,00,000**. Employee/self contribution deductions are **old-regime only**; employer 80CCD(2) is available in both regimes.

⚠️ Employer welfare contributions (PF + NPS + Superannuation) above **₹7,50,000** are taxed as a **perquisite**. Since employer NPS is already added as salary, the excess is effectively **added back twice** with deduction only once.

### 12.3.2 Tax on amounts received (Summary)
| Event | Taxability |
|---|---|
| Accumulation phase | **Tax free** |
| Final withdrawal at closure/exit | **Exempt up to 60% of corpus** (effectively whole withdrawal tax-free) |
| Partial withdrawal (employee) | Exempt up to **25% of employee's contribution** |
| Amount to nominee on death | **Fully exempt** |
| Tier II withdrawal | Taxed as **capital gains** |
| Amount used to buy annuity | No tax on the purchase; the **annuity/pension is taxable** when received |
| NPS Vatsalya partial withdrawal | Exempt up to **25% of contributions** (Sec 10(12BA)) |
| Pension received | **Taxable** as income |

## 12.4 Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT)

A **REIT** is like a mutual fund for income-producing real estate (offices, malls, warehouses). It's a **hybrid pass-through entity**: some income is exempt at REIT level and taxed at the **unit-holder** level.

### 12.4.1 Income types (Sec 115UA)
| Income | At REIT level | At unit-holder level |
|---|---|---|
| **Rental income** | Exempt (10(23FCA)) | **Taxable** (pass-through) |
| **Interest from SPV** | Exempt (10(23FC)) | **Taxable** (pass-through) |
| **Dividend from SPV** | Exempt (10(23FC)) | Taxable, **unless** SPV did NOT opt for 115BAA → then exempt for unit-holder too (10(23FD)) |
| **Capital gains** (on property) | **Taxed at REIT level** | Not taxed |
| **Other income** | Taxed at REIT (max marginal rate) | If not taxed at REIT, taxable to unit-holder under Sec 56(2)(xii) |

### 12.4.3 Capital gain on units of REIT
- **Redemption of units:** taxed as *Other Sources* (Sec 56(2)(xii)).
- **Transfer of units** (other than redemption): capital gain.
  - Listed: long-term after **12 months**; Unlisted: after **24 months**.
  - If STT paid: STCG @**20% (111A)**; LTCG @**12.50% over ₹1.25 lakh (112A)**.
  - If STT not paid: STCG at slab; LTCG @**12.50% (112)**.
  - **Grandfathering** for units bought on/before 31-01-2018.

### 12.4.4 TDS (Sec 194LBA)
| Distributed income | Resident | Non-resident |
|---|---|---|
| Rental | 10% | 30% (foreign co. 35%) |
| Dividend from SPV | 10% | 10% |
| Interest from SPV | 10% | 5% |

Reporting: **Form 64B** to unit-holders, **Form 64A** to the tax department.

## 12.5 InvITs
**Infrastructure Investment Trusts** — same structure and tax as REITs **except**: REIT rental income passes through to unit-holders, but **InvIT rental income is taxed in the InvIT's own hands**, not the unit-holder's.

## 12.6 Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs)
A privately-pooled fund for sophisticated investors (not a mutual fund). Set up as trust/company/LLP, registered with SEBI.

### Category-I & II (Sec 115UB — pass-through)
- **Business income:** taxed **in the AIF's hands** (at company/firm rate, or **maximum marginal rate** if a trust); exempt for unit-holders (10(23FBB)).
- **Any other income:** **exempt at AIF level (10(23FBA))**, taxable in unit-holder's hands, retaining its character and proportion (deemed credited on the last day of the year even if not paid).
- **Losses:** business losses stay with the AIF (carried forward, not passed). Since AY 2020-21, **non-business losses pass through** to unit-holders (if the unit held ≥12 months).
- **TDS** under Sec 194LBB: **10%** (resident), rates in force (foreign). Reporting: **Form 64C** to investors (by 30 June), **Form 64D** to department (by 30 Nov).

**💡 Worked example:** Cat-I AIF (trust) earns Business ₹20L, Capital gains ₹15L, Other sources ₹5L; Mr. X owns 30%.
- AIF taxes its **₹20L business income at MMR**.
- Mr. X: capital gains 30%×15L = ₹4.5L (taxed as capital gains) + other sources 30%×5L = ₹1.5L (deemed credited even if not paid) = **₹6L total**. PGBP share is exempt to him.

### Category-III AIF
**No pass-through.** Taxed like a normal trust/company/LLP (or, if a "specified fund" under 10(4D), gets various exemptions).

## 12.7 Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)
ETFs track an index/commodity but trade like a stock on the exchange.

### Gold ETFs
Taxed like physical gold / non-equity MF.
- Held **>12 months:** LTCG @**12.50%** (the source text mentions indexation, but the post-FA-2024 rule is no indexation on most assets).
- Held **≤12 months:** at slab rate.
- ⚠️ Gold ETF bought **after 01-04-2023 and sold before 01-04-2025:** **always short-term**, slab rate (Section 50AA logic). Doesn't apply if bought before 01-04-2023 or sold on/after 01-04-2025.

**💡 Worked example:** 10,000 Gold ETF units @₹30 (01-03-2018), sold @₹50 (25-03-2025). Held 7 years → **LTCG ₹2,00,000 @12.50%**.

### Equity Index ETFs
Taxed like a listed **equity-oriented mutual fund**: LTCG (>12 mth) @12.50% over ₹1.25 lakh (112A); STCG @20% (111A, STT). *(The text's older "15%" reference is superseded by the 20% rate under the FA-2024 regime.)*

## 12.8 Life Insurance Products

**General rule:** Any sum received from a life insurance policy (including bonus) is **exempt under Section 10(10D)**.

**Exceptions (becomes taxable):**
- (a) Keyman insurance policy proceeds;
- (b) Sec 80DD(3) amount where dependent predeceases the assessee;
- (c) Policy issued **01-04-2003 to 31-03-2012** if premium in any year exceeds **20% of sum assured**;
- (d) Policy issued **on/after 01-04-2012** if premium in any year exceeds **10% of sum assured**.
- **⚠️ Any sum received on death is ALWAYS exempt** — (c) and (d) never apply to death proceeds.

**Endowment policies (on/after 01-04-2023):** if total premium exceeds **₹5,00,000**, maturity proceeds are **taxable as Other Sources** even if the 10%-of-sum-assured test is met (death proceeds still exempt).

**💡 Worked example:** ₹60 lakh endowment policy taken 01-04-2024, premium ₹6 lakh/year. 10% test met (6L = 10% of 60L), but premium >₹5 lakh → maturity proceeds **taxable as Other Sources** (after deducting premiums not previously deducted).

**Section 80C on premiums:** premium deductible up to the ₹1.5 lakh cap. Deduction restricted to **20%** of sum assured (policies ≤31-03-2012), **10%** (on/after 01-04-2012), **15%** for disabled/specified-disease persons (policies on/after 01-04-2013). Minimum holding: **5 years (ULIP), 2 years (life policy)** — early termination claws back earlier deductions.

### ULIPs (Unit Linked Insurance Plans)
- High-value ULIPs issued **on/after 01-02-2021** with **aggregate premium > ₹2,50,000** lose the 10(10D) exemption and are treated as **capital assets**.
- The **equity-oriented fund** inside a high-premium ULIP (maintaining ≥65%/90% equity throughout): long-term period **1 year**; **LTCG @12.50%, STCG @20%**.
- **Non-equity** fund inside a high-premium ULIP: taxed under normal capital-gains rules (slab for short term, 12.50% for long term).
- From FY 2025-26, **all ULIPs without 10(10D) exemption** are capital assets; their maturity proceeds = **capital gains**.
- **Death proceeds remain exempt** under 10(10D).

## 12.9 Reverse Mortgage
A senior citizen mortgages their house to borrow funds while continuing to live in it.
- **Section 47(xvi):** a reverse-mortgage transaction (under a notified Govt scheme) is **NOT a transfer** → no capital gain.
- **Section 10(43):** the loan amount received (lump-sum or instalments) is **exempt from tax**.

## 📌 One-Page Recap
- **ESOP:** perquisite at allotment = (FMV on exercise − price paid) × shares; later capital gain with cost = exercise-date FMV, holding from allotment date. Start-up perquisite tax deferred.
- **SGB:** 2.5% interest (taxed); **individual redemption exempt (Sec 47)**; secondary-market sale taxed (12.50% LTCG > 12 mth).
- **NPS:** 80CCD(1) 10% (in ₹1.5L cap), 80CCD(1B) extra ₹50k, 80CCD(2) employer 10%/14%; final withdrawal 60% exempt; nominee fully exempt; pension/annuity taxable; Tier II = capital gains.
- **REIT:** rental + SPV interest/dividend pass through; capital gains taxed at REIT. **InvIT:** same but rental taxed at InvIT.
- **AIF Cat I/II:** pass-through (business income taxed at AIF); **Cat III:** no pass-through.
- **Gold ETF:** like non-equity gold; **Index ETF:** like equity MF.
- **Life insurance:** exempt 10(10D) unless premium >10%/20% of SA, or endowment premium >₹5L (post-Apr-2023); **death always exempt**.
- **High-value ULIP (>₹2.5L premium, post 01-02-2021):** capital asset; equity fund LTCG 12.50% / STCG 20%.
- **Reverse mortgage:** not a transfer (47(xvi)); loan exempt (10(43)).
