# Chapter 5: Retirement Products — Flashcards

Cover the answer, recall it, then check.

**Q1.** What is EPF and is it mandatory?
> **A:** Employees' Provident Fund — a **mandatory** savings-cum-retirement scheme for employees of eligible organisations, meant as a retirement corpus.

**Q2.** How much do employee and employer each contribute to EPF?
> **A:** Each contributes **12% of (Basic + DA)**.

**Q3.** How is the employer's 12% EPF contribution split?
> **A:** **3.67%** to EPF, **8.33%** to EPS (capped at Rs. 15,000 monthly salary), **0.5%** to EDLIS, **0.5%** admin charges.

**Q4.** When can EPF contribution be 10% instead of 12%?
> **A:** If the company has **<20 employees**, losses exceed net worth, or it's in the **beedi/jute/brick/guar gum/coir** industry.

**Q5.** What special EPF rate applies to new women employees?
> **A:** **8% (instead of 12%)** for the **first 3 years** — but the employer still contributes 12%.

**Q6.** When is EPF interest credited, and who sets the rate?
> **A:** Calculated monthly, **credited on 31st March**; rate set by the **Central Board of Trustees (CBT)** with the Ministry of Finance.

**Q7.** When does interest on EPF own contribution become taxable?
> **A:** When own contribution (EPF + VPF) **exceeds Rs. 2.5 lakh** in a financial year — interest on the excess is taxable.

**Q8.** When is EPF withdrawal fully tax-free?
> **A:** After **5 consecutive years** of service (or if transferred to a new employer; or if exit was beyond the employee's control).

**Q9.** TDS on EPF withdrawal before 5 years if amount > Rs. 50,000?
> **A:** **10% TDS** if PAN furnished; **maximum marginal rate** if not. No TDS if Form 15G/15H submitted.

**Q10.** Does the employer's EPS contribution earn interest, and when is the pension payable?
> **A:** **No interest** on EPS; pension payable only **after age 58**.

**Q11.** What is VPF and is there an employer match?
> **A:** Voluntary Provident Fund — extra contribution above 12% (up to **100% of Basic+DA**) into the EPF account; **no employer match**.

**Q12.** PPF lock-in, limits, and eligibility?
> **A:** **15-year** lock-in; **Rs. 500–1.5 lakh/year**; only **resident Indians** (no NRIs, no joint/multiple accounts).

**Q13.** What does "EEE" mean for PPF?
> **A:** Exempt-Exempt-Exempt: contribution deductible (80C), interest exempt, maturity exempt.

**Q14.** When can you take a loan against PPF and how much?
> **A:** From start of **3rd FY to end of 6th FY**; up to **25%** of balance at end of the 2nd FY preceding the loan year.

**Q15.** Minimum service for gratuity, and the exceptions?
> **A:** **5 years** continuous service; waived on **death or disability**.

**Q16.** When is an organisation "covered" under the Payment of Gratuity Act?
> **A:** If it employs **≥10 persons** on any single day in the preceding 12 months (once covered, always covered).

**Q17.** Gratuity formula for employees covered under the Act?
> **A:** **(15 × last drawn salary × tenure) ÷ 26**; round tenure up if last part-year >6 months.

**Q18.** Gratuity formula for employees NOT covered under the Act?
> **A:** **(15 × last drawn salary × tenure) ÷ 30**; count only fully completed years.

**Q19.** Worked example: Basic Rs. 60,000, 20 yrs 7 months, covered. Gratuity?
> **A:** Tenure rounds to 21; (15 × 60,000 × 21)/26 = **Rs. 7.26 lakh**.

**Q20.** Gratuity tax treatment for government employees?
> **A:** **Fully exempt.**

**Q21.** What is "commutation" in superannuation?
> **A:** Taking a portion (up to **one-third**) of the annuity corpus as a **lump sum**; the balance buys an annuity.

**Q22.** IT cap on employer's combined PF + superannuation contribution?
> **A:** **27% of the employee's earnings.**

**Q23.** Does NPS guarantee returns?
> **A:** **No.** Subscribers earn **market returns**; pension depends on the corpus built.

**Q24.** Difference between NPS Tier I and Tier II accounts?
> **A:** Tier I = default/mandatory, restricted withdrawals, tax benefits. Tier II = voluntary, unrestricted withdrawals, no tax benefit (except govt employees under 80C).

**Q25.** NPS tax deductions available to a subscriber?
> **A:** **80CCD(1)** up to Rs. 1.5 lakh (within 80C), **80CCD(1B)** extra Rs. 50,000, **80CCD(2)** employer's up to 14% of Basic+DA — total subscriber up to **Rs. 2 lakh**.

**Q26.** NPS exit at age 60 — how much must buy an annuity?
> **A:** **Minimum 40%** of corpus buys an annuity; **up to 60%** withdrawn as lump sum. If corpus ≤ Rs. 5 lakh, full withdrawal allowed.

**Q27.** NPS exit before 60 — annuity requirement?
> **A:** **At least 80%** must buy an annuity; 20% lump sum. If corpus ≤ Rs. 2.5 lakh, full withdrawal allowed.

**Q28.** NPS taxation regime?
> **A:** **E, E, partly-Exempt/partly-Taxable**: contributions exempt, returns exempt, 60% lump sum tax-free (10(12A)), 40% annuity taxable as income.

**Q29.** Maximum equity allocation under NPS Active Choice and Auto Choice (Aggressive)?
> **A:** **75%** in both Active Choice and the Aggressive Life Cycle fund. (Moderate = 50%, Conservative = 25%.)

**Q30.** What is the Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) assured payout?
> **A:** **50% of the last 12-month average Basic pay** after min 25 years' service (Central Govt, w.e.f. 1 Apr 2025); min guaranteed Rs. 10,000/month after 10+ years.

**Q31.** Who can join Atal Pension Yojana (APY)?
> **A:** Citizens **18–40** in the unorganised sector, **not** in any statutory social security scheme and **not** income-tax payers.

**Q32.** Two basic types of annuity?
> **A:** **Deferred** (payments begin >12 months later) and **Immediate** (payments begin within 12 months).

**Q33.** Which annuity payout option gives the highest amount, and which the lowest?
> **A:** **Highest** = lifetime *without* return of purchase price; **lowest** = lifetime *with* return of purchase price.

**Q34.** Are annuity payments tax-free?
> **A:** **No** — they are taxed as income in the annuitant's hands; only the **premium** qualifies under 80C.

**Q35.** How does an SWP differ from FD interest?
> **A:** In an FD, taking interest doesn't reduce the corpus; in an SWP, **units are sold**, so the fund value falls. Higher NAV → fewer units sold.

**Q36.** Equity-fund SWP capital gains tax: short vs long term?
> **A:** ≤12 months → **20%**; >12 months → **12.5%** (LTCG exemption Rs. 1.25 lakh/year).

**Q37.** What are the two goals of bond/FD laddering?
> **A:** **Manage interest-rate risk** and **manage cash flow** by staggering maturities.

**Q38.** SCSS eligibility age and maximum deposit?
> **A:** **60+** (55–60 if VRS/superannuation; 50 for Defence); max deposit **Rs. 30 lakh**; matures in 5 years (+3 extension).

**Q39.** What is a reverse mortgage and its max tenure?
> **A:** Pledge an owned home to receive **periodic income** (opposite of a normal mortgage); max tenure **20 years**; receipts exempt under **Section 10(43)**.

**Q40.** When does a reverse mortgage loan become due?
> **A:** Only when the **last surviving borrower dies or permanently moves out**; settled from sale proceeds, surplus to heirs.
