# Chapter 13: Tax Provisions for Special Cases — Flashcards

Cover the answer, recall it, then check.

**Q1.** What is the cost of acquisition of bonus shares?
> **A:** **Nil** (unless allotted before 01-04-2001 → FMV on 01-04-2001 at the assessee's option).

**Q2.** From which date is the holding period of bonus shares counted?
> **A:** From the **date of allotment of the bonus shares** (not the original shares).

**Q3.** Is there any tax when bonus shares are allotted?
> **A:** **No** — neither company nor shareholder is taxed at allotment; tax arises only at sale.

**Q4.** 10,000 shares @₹105 (Apr-2020); 1:2 bonus (Sep-2023); all 15,000 sold @₹120 (Aug-2024). How are the bonus shares taxed?
> **A:** 5,000 bonus shares, cost nil, held ~11 months → **STCG ₹6,00,000 @20% (Sec 111A)**.

**Q5.** Are share split and consolidation treated as a transfer?
> **A:** **No** — not within Section 2(47); no tax at the time, only on later sale.

**Q6.** After a 2:1 consolidation of 2,000 shares (₹2,00,000 total), what is the cost per consolidated share?
> **A:** ₹2,00,000 ÷ 1,000 = **₹200/share**. (Holding period from the original acquisition date.)

**Q7.** How is buyback taxed for the shareholder from 01-10-2024?
> **A:** The **entire buyback proceeds** are taxed as **dividend** (Income from Other Sources); no deduction; the **cost becomes a capital loss**.

**Q8.** How was buyback taxed before 01-10-2024?
> **A:** The **company** paid 20% (+12% surcharge +4% cess); the shareholder's proceeds were **exempt** under Section 10(34A).

**Q9.** In a post-Oct-2024 buyback of 500 shares (cost ₹100 each, bought-back @₹125), what happens to the ₹50,000 cost?
> **A:** It becomes a **capital loss** (long-term or short-term per holding) available for set-off/carry-forward. The ₹62,500 received is taxed as dividend.

**Q10.** What is the cost of acquisition of a renounced rights entitlement?
> **A:** **Nil.**

**Q11.** Is gain on renouncing a rights entitlement taxed under Section 111A?
> **A:** **No** — a right is not a listed equity share, so it's taxed at **normal slab rates** (usually short-term).

**Q12.** When a shareholder exercises rights and buys shares, what is the cost of those shares?
> **A:** The **price paid to the company** (plus, for a buyer of the right, any amount paid to the renouncer). Holding from the date of allotment.

**Q13.** Is allotment of shares in an amalgamation a transfer?
> **A:** **No** — Section 47(vii) excludes it (if held as a capital asset). Stock-in-trade is taxed as business income.

**Q14.** After an amalgamation, what cost and holding period apply to the new shares when sold?
> **A:** **Cost = amount paid for the original shares**; **holding period from the original acquisition date**.

**Q15.** What % of amalgamating-company shareholders must become shareholders of the amalgamated company?
> **A:** At least **75% in value**.

**Q16.** Is lending shares under SLB a transfer?
> **A:** **No** — Section 47(xv); no capital gain even if the exact scrips aren't returned.

**Q17.** How is the lender's SLB fee taxed?
> **A:** Under **PGBP** (if a business) or **Income from Other Sources**, with related expenses deductible.

**Q18.** Who is the Authorised Intermediary for SLB?
> **A:** The **Clearing Corporation of NSE/BSE**; only F&O-eligible securities and liquid index ETFs qualify; tenure ≤12 months.

**Q19.** How is the borrower in SLB taxed?
> **A:** Gains/losses on the bought/sold stock are **capital gains or PGBP**; the lending fee paid is deductible.

**Q20.** Is conversion of preference shares into equity of the same company a transfer?
> **A:** **No** — Section 47(xb); no capital gain at conversion.

**Q21.** After converting preference shares to equity, what cost and holding apply on sale?
> **A:** **Cost = cost of the preference shares**; **holding period from the date the preference shares were acquired**.

**Q22.** What is a segregated portfolio (side pocket)?
> **A:** A separate scheme carved out of an MF for debt/money-market instruments that hit a **credit event**; unit-holders then hold units in both the main and segregated schemes.

**Q23.** How is the cost of segregated-portfolio units computed?
> **A:** Cost of total portfolio × (NAV of asset moved to segregated portfolio ÷ NAV of total portfolio just before segregation); this is subtracted from the main portfolio's cost.

**Q24.** 1,000 units cost ₹15,000; segregated NAV ₹2, total NAV ₹12. Cost of segregated units?
> **A:** 15,000 × (2/12) = **₹2,500** (₹2.5/unit); main portfolio cost becomes ₹12,500.

**Q25.** For segregated units, how is the holding period determined?
> **A:** It **includes** the period the original units were held in the main portfolio.

**Q26.** Is consolidation of two mutual fund schemes a transfer?
> **A:** **No** (if both are equity-oriented or both non-equity); cost and holding period of the consolidating scheme carry over.

**Q27.** How is the amount received on winding up of a mutual fund scheme taxed?
> **A:** Like a **normal redemption** — capital gains as usual.

**Q28.** What is the LTCG exemption limit under Section 112A?
> **A:** **₹1,25,000** per year; excess @12.50%.

**Q29.** What is the surcharge cap on capital gains under 111A/112/112A?
> **A:** **15%** (even above ₹2 crore), unlike other income which can reach 25%/37%.

**Q30.** Tax rate on Virtual Digital Assets (Section 115BBH) and on undisclosed income (Section 115BBE)?
> **A:** VDA = **30%**; undisclosed income = **60%**.

**Q31.** Is indexation available on capital assets now?
> **A:** **No** (Finance Act 2024) — except immovable property in certain cases (12.5% without indexation vs 20% with, for property bought before 23-07-2024).

**Q32.** Can short-term capital loss be set off against long-term capital gains?
> **A:** **Yes** — STCL can be set off against both short-term and long-term capital gains (whereas LTCL only against LTCG).
