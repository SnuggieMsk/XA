## 📋 What this exam is about (read this first!)

Think of an **Investment Adviser** as a financial doctor. A doctor checks your body and tells you what medicine to take. An Investment Adviser checks your **money situation** — your income, your goals (buying a house, kid's college, retirement), your fears about losing money — and tells you which financial "medicines" (insurance, mutual funds, pension plans, etc.) are right for *you*.

The **Level 2 exam (Series X-B)** tests whether you can give *complete, advanced* advice. It focuses on five big areas:

1. **Insurance** — protecting people from money disasters (death, accident, illness, fire).
2. **Retirement planning** — making sure people have money when they stop working.
3. **Taxation** — legally paying the least tax on different investments.
4. **Estate planning** — deciding who gets your money/property after you die (wills, trusts).
5. **Behavioural finance & risk profiling** — understanding the *psychology* of why people make money mistakes.

## 📝 Exam Structure (very important — plan your time!)

| Part | What | Marks |
|------|------|-------|
| **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)** | 90 questions × 1 mark | **90** |
| **Case-based Questions** | 6 cases × 5 sub-questions × 2 marks | **60** |
| **TOTAL** | | **150** |

- ⏱️ **Time:** 3 hours
- ✅ **Passing score:** 60% (i.e., **90 out of 150 marks**)
- ⚠️ **Negative marking:** 25% of the marks for that question. So a wrong 1-mark MCQ costs you **−0.25**; a wrong 2-mark case answer costs you **−0.5**.
- 🧮 **Calculator note:** Test computers have **Excel / OpenOffice Calc** — practise doing tax and return calculations in a spreadsheet.

> **Smart strategy:** Because of negative marking, only guess if you can eliminate at least 2 options. The **case studies are worth 60 marks (40% of the exam)** and are mostly *numerical* (tax, capital gains, retirement corpus). Chapter 20 is dedicated to these — don't skip it!

## 🧠 The 6 Numbers You Must Memorise Cold

These show up again and again — burn them into memory:

1. **Pass mark:** 60% = 90/150
2. **Negative marking:** 25% of the question's marks
3. **LTCG on listed equity/equity MF:** taxed at **12.5%** above the **₹1.25 lakh** yearly exemption (no indexation)
4. **STCG on listed equity/equity MF (Sec 111A):** **20%**
5. **Section 80C limit:** **₹1.5 lakh** per year
6. **Holding period to be "long-term":** **12 months** for listed equity/equity MF & listed securities; **24 months** for immovable property & unlisted shares.
