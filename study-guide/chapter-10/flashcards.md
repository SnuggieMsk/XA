# Chapter 10: Taxation of Debt Products — Flashcards

Cover the answer, recall it, then check.

**Q1.** What are the two types of income earned from debt products, and under which heads are they taxed?
> **A:** (1) **Interest income** — taxed under *Income from Other Sources* (unless business). (2) **Capital gains** on transfer/redemption — taxed under *Capital Gains*.

**Q2.** Under Section 145, what decides whether interest is taxed on accrual or receipt?
> **A:** The **method of accounting regularly employed**: mercantile (accrual) system → taxed when due; cash system → taxed when received.

**Q3.** Are securities held by FPIs treated as capital assets or stock-in-trade?
> **A:** **Always capital assets** — so FPI gains are always taxed as capital gains.

**Q4.** What is the LTCG rate on a listed bond and the holding period to qualify?
> **A:** **12.50%** (no indexation), if held **more than 12 months**.

**Q5.** How are gains on an UNLISTED bond/debenture taxed?
> **A:** **Always short-term capital gains at normal slab rates**, regardless of holding period.

**Q6.** A bond is sold for ₹13,000 including ₹75 accrued interest. How do you compute capital gain?
> **A:** Subtract the ₹75 (taxed separately as interest) from the sale price first → adjusted consideration ₹12,925, then deduct cost. Interest is never taxed as capital gain.

**Q7.** What is Yield-to-Maturity (YTM)?
> **A:** The discount rate at which the present value of a bond's future cash flows equals its market price.

**Q8.** Name the four types of Government Securities.
> **A:** Cash Management Bills (CMBs), Treasury Bills (T-Bills), Dated G-Secs, and State Development Loans (SDLs).

**Q9.** How are T-Bills and CMBs taxed?
> **A:** They carry no interest; the discount earned on redemption/transfer is a **short-term capital gain** at applicable rates (maturity < 1 year).

**Q10.** How are Dated G-Secs and SDLs taxed?
> **A:** Same as **listed bonds** — LTCG @12.50% if held >12 months and listed.

**Q11.** When does TDS apply on interest from Government securities to a resident?
> **A:** Generally no TDS under Section 193 — **except** 8% Savings (Taxable) Bonds 2003 and 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds 2018, and only if interest in the year exceeds **₹10,000**.

**Q12.** For tax-free bonds, what is taxable and what is exempt?
> **A:** **Interest is exempt** (Section 10); **capital gains on transfer/redemption are taxable** (same as coupon bonds).

**Q13.** How many mutual fund tax categories exist from FY 2023-24, and name them.
> **A:** **Four:** (1) Equity-Oriented, (2) SMF Type 1, (3) SMF Type 2, (4) Other Mutual Funds.

**Q14.** Define a Specified Mutual Fund Type 1.
> **A:** A fund investing **≥65%** in debt/money-market instruments (directly or indirectly), **bought on/after 01-04-2023**.

**Q15.** Define a Specified Mutual Fund Type 2.
> **A:** A fund (not Type 1) that invested **<35%** in Indian equity, bought **on/after 01-04-2023 but sold before 01-04-2025** (e.g. debt-hybrids, gold/silver, international, FoF).

**Q16.** How are capital gains on SMF Type 1 and Type 2 taxed (Section 50AA)?
> **A:** **Always short-term, regardless of holding period**, at normal slab rates.

**Q17.** How is dividend from a mutual fund taxed for a resident vs a non-resident?
> **A:** **Resident:** slab rates, with interest-expense deduction capped at **20% of dividend**. **Non-resident:** flat **20%** (subject to DTAA), **no deduction at all**.

**Q18.** A resident gets ₹5,00,000 MF dividend and paid ₹1,50,000 interest to invest. Taxable dividend?
> **A:** Deduction capped at 20% × 5,00,000 = ₹1,00,000 → taxable **₹4,00,000** at slab rates.

**Q19.** What are the long-term holding periods for "Other Mutual Funds" — listed vs unlisted?
> **A:** **Listed: 12 months; Unlisted: 24 months.** LTCG @12.50% either way.

**Q20.** What is an MLD and how is it taxed from FY 2023-24?
> **A:** A **Market Linked Debenture** — return tied to a market index/security. Under Section 50AA, gains on/after 01-04-2023 are **always short-term** at slab rates, even if acquired earlier.

**Q21.** Two benefits denied under Section 50AA?
> **A:** (1) No benefit of holding period (always STCG); (2) **Cost of improvement taken as nil**.

**Q22.** What is a DTAA and how does it help a non-resident?
> **A:** A **Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement** — the NR pays tax at the DTAA rate or Income Tax Act rate, **whichever is more beneficial**, avoiding double taxation.

**Q23.** What documents must a non-resident furnish to claim DTAA benefits?
> **A:** A **Tax Residency Certificate (TRC)** and **Form 10F** (self-declaration).

**Q24.** Under India-USA DTAA, at what rate is interest income taxed (per the workbook example)?
> **A:** A concessional **15%** (Article 11), with foreign tax credit in the USA.

**Q25.** Under Chapter XII-A, what are the tax caps for an eligible NRI?
> **A:** Investment income capped at **20%**, LTCG at **12.50%**, **no indexation**.

**Q26.** Under Chapter XII-A, what happens if LTCG is reinvested within 6 months?
> **A:** It becomes **exempt** (fully if all net consideration reinvested, proportionately if partial). But if the new asset is sold within **3 years**, the exempted gain is taxed then.

**Q27.** What does Section 89A address?
> **A:** Income from **overseas retirement accounts** (USA, Canada, UK) is taxed **on withdrawal**, not on accrual — removing a double-tax mismatch.

**Q28.** Is redemption of a Sovereign Gold Bond by an individual taxable?
> **A:** **No** — Section 47 says redemption by an individual is not a "transfer," so no capital gain arises (interest, however, is taxable).

**Q29.** Can grandfathering or indexation reduce LTCG on debt products under the post-2023 rules?
> **A:** No — LTCG on bonds/G-Secs is taxed at 12.50% **without indexation**.

**Q30.** What is the difference between a Principal Protected and Principal Non-Protected MLD?
> **A:** Principal Protected guarantees return of the principal at maturity; Non-Protected does not — the principal itself can be lost depending on the underlying's performance.
