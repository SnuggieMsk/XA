# Chapter 19: Comparison of Products Across Categories — Flashcards

> **How to use:** Cover the answer, recall it out loud, then check.

**Q1.** What are the three broad parameters used to compare all investment instruments?
> **A:** **Risk, Return, and Liquidity** (often extended with tax and lock-in).

**Q2.** For which products is performance-data disclosure mandated by SEBI?
> **A:** **Mutual Funds and Portfolio Management Services (PMS).** Aggregators then provide comparison services, often free.

**Q3.** What is ELSS and its lock-in?
> **A:** Equity Linked Savings Scheme — a tax-saving MF investing **only in equities**; **3-year lock-in** (shortest among 80C options), then redeemable anytime. Capital gains taxed at **12.50%**.

**Q4.** Compare risk of capital loss: ELSS vs PPF vs NPS Tier 1.
> **A:** ELSS = **high** (all equity); PPF = **low** (sovereign); NPS = **moderate** (can be lowered with more G-secs).

**Q5.** PPF: tax status, lock-in, annual limit?
> **A:** Returns **tax-free**; **15-year** lock-in (partial withdrawal after 5 years); max **Rs.1,50,000/year**.

**Q6.** What happens to the NPS corpus at age 60?
> **A:** **60% can be withdrawn tax-free**; the remaining **40% must buy an annuity** from an insurer, and the **annuity income is fully taxable**.

**Q7.** Minimum investment: Mutual Fund vs PMS vs AIF Cat 3?
> **A:** MF: as low as **Rs.1,000**; PMS: **Rs.50 lakh**; AIF Cat 3: **Rs.1 crore**.

**Q8.** How are PMS operating expenses and upfront charges regulated?
> **A:** **No upfront charge allowed**; operating expenses capped at **0.50%**; the fee can be fixed, performance-based, or both.

**Q9.** Where are PMS investments held vs mutual fund units?
> **A:** PMS = in the **investor's own demat account** (can be customised); MF = pooled vehicle with units allocated to each investor.

**Q10.** Who manages ULIPs, and what is their purpose vs mutual funds?
> **A:** **Life insurance companies** manage ULIPs (insurance **cum** investment); mutual funds are managed by AMCs for **investment only**.

**Q11.** When is a ULIP maturity tax-free?
> **A:** If **sum assured ≥ 10× annual premium**. BUT for ULIPs issued **on/after 1 Feb 2021**, **no exemption** if any year's premium **exceeds Rs.2.5 lakh** (Finance Act 2021, Sec 10(10D)).

**Q12.** ULIP liquidity / lock-in?
> **A:** **5 years** compulsory contribution (except single-premium); no redemption for 5 years; discontinuation charges if premiums stop early.

**Q13.** Active fund vs index fund — goal and cost?
> **A:** Active fund tries to **beat the benchmark (alpha)** at **higher cost**; index fund **replicates** the benchmark at **lower cost**.

**Q14.** Direct equity vs equity funds — risk?
> **A:** Direct equity = **concentrated** risk on chosen stocks; equity funds **spread** risk across many securities (diversified).

**Q15.** ETF vs index fund — pricing and liquidity in India?
> **A:** ETF trades **live (intraday)** on the exchange; index fund priced at **end-of-day NAV**. In India, ETF liquidity is **often poor** (wide bid–ask spreads, NAV-price gaps) except a couple of ETFs.

**Q16.** Which gold form pays interest, and how much?
> **A:** **Sovereign Gold Bonds** — **2.50% p.a.**, paid semi-annually.

**Q17.** Tax on Sovereign Gold Bonds at redemption?
> **A:** **No tax if redeemed with RBI**; capital gains if sold in the market before maturity; the interest is taxable.

**Q18.** Which gold forms incur GST? Which don't?
> **A:** Physical gold, Gold Funds and SGBs involve GST (on the gold bought); **Gold ETF incurs no GST cost** to the buyer.

**Q19.** SGB redemption timeline?
> **A:** Redeemable on anniversary dates **after 5 years**, final redemption after **8 years**; sellable in the market in between (moderate liquidity).

**Q20.** Real estate vs REITs/InvITs — exposure and liquidity?
> **A:** Real estate = **large, concentrated, illiquid**, lump-sum only; **REITs/InvITs** = spread over many assets, lower minimums, **better liquidity** (may be listed), professionally managed.

**Q21.** Company deposit vs company debenture (same company) — risk and return?
> **A:** Deposit is **unsecured → higher risk, slightly higher return**; debenture is normally **secured → lower risk, slightly lower return**.

**Q22.** Safety: Bank FD vs open-ended debt fund vs debt instrument vs FMP?
> **A:** Bank FD = **high** safety; debt funds & FMPs get diversification benefit; single debt instrument = depends on the instrument. FMP liquidity is **very poor**.

**Q23.** Why can a debt fund's growth option give better post-tax returns?
> **A:** Withdrawals from a growth scheme contain a **large element of capital** (not just income), improving the post-tax outcome.

**Q24.** Maximum loss for an option buyer?
> **A:** Limited to the **premium paid**.

**Q25.** Time horizon: index futures vs index funds?
> **A:** Index futures = a **few days to max 3 months**; index funds = **very long / lifetime** (an actual investment).

**Q26.** Market-linked vs non-market-linked retirement products — liquidity?
> **A:** Both have lock-ins to ensure funds are available for retirement; **non-market-linked** (PPF) liquidity tends to be even **poorer**.

**Q27.** Personal Accident insurance vs Life insurance — what death does each cover?
> **A:** Personal Accident covers death due to **accident only**; Life insurance covers death due to **any cause**.

**Q28.** Does life insurance cover disability? Does personal accident?
> **A:** Life insurance does **not** cover disability; **Personal Accident does** (temporary/permanent, total/partial disability from accident).

**Q29.** How should personal accident insurance be positioned?
> **A:** As an **add-on** over adequate life cover (especially for permanent total disability), **not** a substitute for life insurance.

**Q30.** What is the LRS-relevant point for ULIP tax threshold to remember?
> **A:** (ULIP, not LRS) The premium threshold for exemption is **Rs.2.5 lakh** for policies issued on/after 1 Feb 2021; the sum-assured rule is **≥10× premium**.
