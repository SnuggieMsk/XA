# Chapter 19: Comparison of Products Across Categories — Short Notes

## 🎯 What you'll learn
- Where **performance data** for investment products comes from.
- How to **compare products** across the three big yardsticks: **Risk, Return, Liquidity** (plus tax and lock-in).
- Side-by-side comparisons of the main product categories an adviser deals with.

> **The three universal yardsticks:** Every investment is judged on **Risk** (chance of losing money), **Return** (how much it earns), and **Liquidity** (how quickly you can turn it into cash without a big loss). Add **Tax** and **Lock-in** and you can compare almost anything. Remember: usually **higher return comes with higher risk and/or lower liquidity** — you rarely get all three.

---

## 19.1 Performance data for investment products
- Performance data is now **widely available**. For **Mutual Funds and PMS**, disclosure is **mandated by SEBI**.
- **Information aggregators** collect this and offer comparison services on websites — often **free**, with some **paid** value-added services.

## 19.2 Comparing investment alternatives
All instruments are compared on **3 broad parameters: Risk, Return, Liquidity.**

### 19.2.1 ELSS vs other tax-saving instruments
> **ELSS** (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) = a mutual fund that invests **only in equities** and gives a tax deduction. **PPF** = Public Provident Fund (government scheme). **NPS Tier 1** = a pension scheme.

| Parameter | **ELSS** | **PPF / Tax-saving Bank FD** | **NPS — Tier 1** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Invests **only in equities** | PPF = govt scheme; Tax-saving FD = bank deposit | MF-like pension; equity up to **75%** (selected subscribers), G-secs, corporate bonds, Alt. Investments (max 5% for some) |
| **Risk of capital loss** | **High** (all equity) | PPF = **Low** (sovereign); FD = moderately low (bank-risk only) | **Moderate**; can be made low by choosing more G-secs |
| **Returns** | No guarantee; moderately high to high; market-linked. **Capital gains taxed at 12.50%** | PPF = guaranteed-ish (varies with G-sec rate, can change each quarter), **tax-free**, moderate post-tax. Max **Rs.1,50,000/yr**. FD = fixed but **taxable yearly**, moderately low post-tax | No guarantee; moderate to moderately high; market-linked. **60% of corpus tax-free at age 60**; the other **40% must buy an annuity** (annuity income is **fully taxable**) |
| **Liquidity / Lock-in** | **3-year lock-in**, then withdraw anytime | PPF = **15-year** lock-in (partial withdrawal after 5 yrs); FD = locked for the tenure | Locked **till age 60**; partial withdrawals allowed (conditions); premature exit → **80%** of the amount must buy an annuity |

💡 ELSS has the **shortest lock-in (3 years)** of the common 80C tax-savers.

### 19.2.2 Equity Mutual Funds vs PMS vs AIF Category 3
> **PMS** = Portfolio Management Services (a customised, big-ticket managed account). **AIF** = Alternative Investment Fund (complex strategies). "Cat 3" AIFs can use derivatives/leverage.

| Parameter | **Equity Mutual Fund** | **PMS** | **AIF Cat 3** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Pooled vehicle; units allocated to each investor | Investments held in the **individual's own demat account**; **can be customised** | Pooled vehicle; **complex/derivative strategies**, common across investors |
| **Minimum investment** | As low as **Rs. 1,000** | **Rs. 50 lakh** | **Rs. 1 crore** |
| **Concentration / regulation** | SEBI regulates → broad-basing | **Not SEBI-capped** → can be concentrated | SEBI regulates → avoids single-company concentration |
| **Fees** | **Max fee capped by SEBI** (varies by fund size) | No upfront charge; operating expenses capped at **0.50%**; fee can be fixed/performance/both | **Not SEBI-regulated**; as per AIF–investor agreement |
| **For whom** | **Retail investor** | **HNI (High Net-worth Investor)** | HNI seeking complex/leveraged strategies |
| **Redemption** | Anytime (open-ended), possible **exit load** | Manager may charge for redeeming/handing back | **Close-ended AIFs** have lock-in & **poor liquidity** |

⚠️ Remember the minimums: **MF Rs.1,000 → PMS Rs.50 lakh → AIF Rs.1 crore.**

### 19.2.3 Mutual Funds vs ULIPs
> **ULIP** = Unit Linked Insurance Plan — an **insurance + investment** product from a life insurer.

| Parameter | **Mutual Fund** | **ULIP** |
|---|---|---|
| **Managed by** | Asset Management Companies | **Life Insurance Companies** |
| **Purpose** | Investment only | **Insurance cum investment** |
| **Charges** | Capped by **SEBI** | Capped by **IRDAI**; **mortality + other charges deducted from fund value** → post-expense return hard to compute |
| **Returns** | Market-dependent | Market-dependent |
| **Tax on withdrawal** | Taxable per tax laws | Maturity **tax-free if sum assured ≥ 10× annual premium**. BUT **Finance Act 2021** amended **Sec 10(10D)**: **no exemption** for ULIPs issued **on/after 1 Feb 2021 if any year's premium > Rs. 2.5 lakh**. Below that → still exempt |
| **Disclosure** | **Transparent** daily/periodic; many comparison services | **Lower** disclosure; fewer comparison services |
| **Liquidity** | Open-ended → redeem anytime (maybe exit load) | **Compulsory 5-year** contribution (except single premium); **no redemption for 5 years**; discontinuation charges if premiums stop early |

💡 Two ULIP tax numbers to memorise: **sum assured ≥ 10× premium** for exemption, and the **Rs. 2.5 lakh** premium threshold (post-1 Feb 2021).

### 19.2.4 Actively Managed Funds vs Index Funds
| Parameter | **Active Fund** | **Index Fund** |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Beat the benchmark (generate **alpha**) by picking securities | **Replicate** the benchmark (same securities, same proportion) |
| **Cost** | **Higher** (active decision-making) | **Lower** |
| **Return** | Aim for higher return *net of higher cost* | Track the benchmark as closely as possible |

> **Alpha** = the extra return above the benchmark.

### 19.2.5 Direct Equity vs Equity Funds
| Parameter | **Direct Equity** | **Equity Funds** |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Buying specific stocks yourself | Buying an equity fund scheme |
| **Expertise needed** | Personal expertise, or a PMS/RIA | Just pick the right scheme (incl. index funds) |
| **Risk** | **Concentrated** on the chosen stocks | **Spread** across many securities → diversified |

### 19.2.6 ETFs vs Index Funds
> **ETF** = Exchange Traded Fund — an index fund that **trades on the stock exchange** like a share.

| Parameter | **ETF (Exchange Traded Index Fund)** | **Index Fund** |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Index fund **traded on exchange** like a security | Bought/sold **from the AMC** |
| **Price** | Live, **minute-to-minute** | End-of-day **NAV** |
| **Costs** | Management cost low, BUT brokerage, demat, and **buy–sell spread** add cost | Management cost slightly higher, but **no other costs** |
| **Liquidity** | Depends on the market — on Indian exchanges, **mostly poor** (except a few ETFs); big NAV vs price gaps and wide spreads | Available from AMC on **end-of-day** basis |

⚠️ Indian ETF trap: **liquidity is usually poor** with large bid–ask spreads, except a couple of ETFs.

### 19.2.7 Physical Gold vs Gold Funds vs Gold ETFs vs Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)
| Parameter | **Physical Gold** | **Gold Fund** | **Gold ETF** | **Sovereign Gold Bond** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Prevailing price now | End-of-day NAV | Live (intraday) | Priced on last-3-days average at each issue |
| **GST cost** | **Yes** | Yes (on fund's gold; held as asset, not loaded as cost) | **No** | Yes (on fund's gold; as asset) |
| **Purity concerns** | Yes (reduced by hallmarking) | Ensured by fund house | Ensured by fund house | **None** (no physical gold) |
| **Storage/safety cost** | **Yes** | Built into fund charges | Nominal | **None** |
| **Income** | No | No | No | **2.50% p.a. interest**, paid semi-annually |
| **Tax on sale** | Capital gains | Capital gains | Capital gains | **No tax if redeemed with RBI**; capital gains if sold in market before maturity; interest is taxable |
| **Liquidity** | Sell in market | Redeem at EOD NAV | Sellable on exchange, decent liquidity (costs apply) | Redeemable on anniversaries **after 5 yrs**, final after **8 yrs**; sellable in market (moderate liquidity) |

💡 SGB is the only gold form that **pays interest (2.50%)** AND can be **tax-free if held to RBI redemption**.

### 19.2.8 Real Estate vs REITs vs InvITs
> **REIT** = Real Estate Investment Trust (invests in **commercial properties** → rent). **InvIT** = Infrastructure Investment Trust (invests in **infrastructure** like roads/bridges).
>
> ⚠️ Note the source's description: REITs → "primarily infrastructure projects like roads, bridges" and InvITs → "primarily real estate commercial properties." (Standard market definitions are the reverse; for the exam, follow the workbook's table wording.)

| Parameter | **Real Estate** | **REITs** | **InvITs** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Exposure** | Large, **concentrated**, lump-sum only | Spread over many properties; **lower minimum** possible | Spread over many projects; lower minimum possible |
| **Management** | By the investor | By the investment manager | By the investment manager |
| **Liquidity** | **Poor**, time-consuming | Better (may be listed) | Better (may be listed) |

### 19.2.9 Debt Instruments vs Open-ended Debt Fund vs FMP vs Bank FD
> **FMP** = Fixed Maturity Plan — a closed-ended debt fund with a fixed tenure.

| Parameter | **Debt Instruments** | **Open-ended Debt Fund** | **FMP** | **Bank FD** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Safety** | Depends on instrument | Diversification → relatively higher safety for same rating | Same (diversified) | **High** |
| **Returns** | In proportion to risk | Post-tax can be better via growth option (withdrawals have large capital element) | Pre-defined if held to maturity | Pre-defined |
| **Liquidity** | **Poor** pre-maturity; selling costs a lot (much lower price) | Redeemable from fund (possible exit load) | **Very poor** (listed but illiquid) | **Instant** (but interest clawback / premature penalty) |

### 19.2.10 Index Futures vs Index Options vs Index Funds
| Parameter | **Index Futures** | **Index Options** | **Index Funds** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time horizon** | Days to **max 3 months** | Days to ~3 months (long-dated also exist) | **Very long / lifetime** (actual investment) |
| **Cost** | Interest cost for delayed purchase | Interest cost **+ risk premium** (option is discretionary) | Market cost + ongoing management charge |
| **Risk** | Same as buying an index fund | **Max loss = premium paid** | Risk of the underlying securities |

### 19.2.11 Gold Futures vs Gold Options vs Gold ETFs vs Gold Fund
| Parameter | **Gold Futures** | **Gold Options** | **Gold ETF** | **Gold Fund** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Horizon** | 12 months | Per contract calendar | Long/lifetime | Long/lifetime |
| **Cost** | Interest cost for delay | Interest cost **+ risk premium** | Market cost + AMC charge | Market cost + AMC charge |
| **Risk** | Same as buying index fund | **Max loss = premium** | Gold price fluctuation | Gold price fluctuation |

### 19.2.12 Company Deposits vs Company Debentures (same company)
| Parameter | **Company Deposit** | **Company Debenture** |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | **Unsecured** → relatively **higher** risk | Normally **secured** → relatively **lower** risk |
| **Return** | Slightly **higher** (compensates for higher risk) | Slightly **lower** |
| **Liquidity** | Company redeems (no interest if redeemed within a year) | Market-dependent (liquidity always an issue) |

💡 Logic: more risk (unsecured deposit) → more return; more safety (secured debenture) → less return.

## 19.3 Other comparatives

### 19.3.1 Market-linked vs Non-market-linked Retirement Products
| Parameter | **Market-linked (NPS, ELSS, MF schemes)** | **Non-market-linked (PPF, etc.)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | Depends on option chosen; linked to market (G-sec option in NPS = low; ELSS = high) | **Low** for return of principal |
| **Returns** | Linked to market, vary by option | Moderate to low (even EPF/PPF now reset returns yearly/quarterly) |
| **Liquidity** | Lock-ins (so funds are actually there for retirement) | Even **poorer** liquidity (principal-guaranteed, same purpose) |

### 19.3.2 Personal Accident Insurance vs Life Insurance
| Parameter | **Personal Accident Insurance** | **Life Insurance** |
|---|---|---|
| **Covers death due to** | **Accident only** | **Any cause** (including accident) |
| **Disability cover** | Yes — temporary/permanent, total/partial disability from accident | **No** |
| **Suitability** | An **add-on** over adequate life cover, especially for permanent total disability from accident | **Essential** for income earners with dependents |

💡 Personal accident is a **supplement**, not a substitute, for life insurance.

## 📌 One-Page Recap
- Compare everything on **Risk, Return, Liquidity** (+ tax + lock-in). High return ↔ high risk / low liquidity.
- **ELSS:** all-equity, high risk, **3-yr lock-in** (shortest 80C); gains taxed 12.50%. **PPF:** sovereign, tax-free, 15-yr lock-in, max Rs.1.5 lakh/yr. **NPS:** 60% tax-free at 60, 40% → annuity (taxable).
- Minimums: **MF Rs.1,000 → PMS Rs.50 lakh → AIF Rs.1 crore.**
- **ULIP** tax: exempt if SA ≥ 10× premium; **but** no exemption post-1 Feb 2021 if premium > **Rs.2.5 lakh**.
- **Active vs Index:** index = lower cost, tracks benchmark; active chases **alpha** at higher cost.
- **ETF vs Index Fund:** ETF trades live but Indian ETF liquidity often **poor** (wide spreads).
- **Gold:** SGB pays **2.50%** interest, **tax-free if redeemed with RBI**, no GST/storage.
- **Real estate** = concentrated, illiquid; **REITs/InvITs** = diversified, more liquid.
- **Company deposit** (unsecured, higher return) vs **debenture** (secured, lower return).
- **Personal accident** = add-on (accident + disability); **life insurance** = essential, any-cause death, no disability cover.
